# Product Principles

Version: 1.0

---

## Principle 1

Architecture First

Design before coding.

---

## Principle 2

Keep it simple.

Avoid unnecessary abstraction.

---

## Principle 3

Every feature starts with a Spec.

---

## Principle 4

Every feature must have Unit Tests.

---

## Principle 5

Business Logic does not belong in UI.

---

## Principle 6

UI never directly accesses Repository or Database.

---

## Principle 7

Preview Models are UI Models.

They are never Domain Entities.

---

## Principle 8

Repository abstracts persistence.

Application layer never depends on SQLite directly.

---

## Principle 9

Technical Debt is documented.

It is not silently ignored.

---

## Principle 10

Every Milestone ends with:

- Architecture Review
- Release Note
- Technical Debt Review
